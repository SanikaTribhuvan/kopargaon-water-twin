### 🪧 SLIDE 5: MULTI-CRITERIA QUESTION PAPER ASSEMBLER ALGORITHM
* **Header:** Mathematical Formulation of Balanced Assessment
* **Constraint Optimization Formulation (Multi-Dimensional Knapsack):**
  $$\text{Maximize } \sum_{i \in \text{Selected}} \text{QualityScore}(i)$$
* **Subject to Hard Constraints:**
  * **Exact Marks Constraint:** $\sum \text{Marks}(i) = 100 \text{ Marks}$ (or 70/50 Marks)
  * **Syllabus Unit Uniformity:** $\text{UnitMarks}(u) = \frac{\text{Total Marks}}{\text{Total Units}} \pm 5\%$
  * **Cognitive Bloom's Ratio:**
    $$\text{L1/L2 (Knowledge/Understand)} = 30\% \quad | \quad \text{L3/L4 (Apply/Analyze)} = 50\% \quad | \quad \text{L5/L6 (Evaluate/Create)} = 20\%$$
  * **Difficulty Index:** $\text{Easy (30\%)} + \text{Medium (50\%)} + \text{Hard (20\%)}$
  * **Sub-Topic Orthogonality:** No two selected questions test the identical sub-clause (zero redundancy).

> **🎙️ Speaker Notes (Slide 5 - 45s):**  
> *"Assembling a question paper is a complex multi-dimensional knapsack optimization problem. Our algorithm selects questions from the bank that satisfy total marks, unit-wise distribution, difficulty ratios, and Bloom's cognitive taxonomy simultaneously in under 500 milliseconds, eliminating human bias and manual errors."*

* **Header:** Fair & Cheat-Proof Multi-Set Examination
* **The Challenge:** In physical exam halls, students sitting next to each other copy answers if papers are identical, but different sets often have unequal difficulty.
* **The PaperTrail Solution – Isomorphic Equivalence:**

  * **Intelligent Distractor Randomization:** For objective/MCQ sections, question order and answer options are randomized using seeded pseudo-random permutations.
  * Guarantees that no student receives an unfair advantage or disadvantage.

> **🎙️ Speaker Notes (Slide 6 - 40s):**  
> *"To prevent exam hall cheating, PaperTrail generates Sets A, B, C, and D. But unlike manual setters who accidentally make Set B harder than Set A, our system mathematically guarantees isomorphic equivalence—meaning all four sets have the exact same cognitive difficulty and syllabus balance."*
